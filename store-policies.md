### Monthly subscriptions

- Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime and won't renew the following month.

### How to request a refund

Email **support.grovely@gmail.com** with your order number (from your Lemon Squeezy receipt). Refunds go back to your original payment method, typically within **5–10 business days**.
